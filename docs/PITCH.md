# 150-word pitch

Stetho is a stethoscope for small pumps and motors, delivered through WhatsApp. A farmer or mechanic rests a phone near a running pump and sends a short video. Stetho checks recording quality, cancels hand shake using the still background, and extracts sound and motion evidence. Its key idea is comparing every check with **that machine's own healthy recordings** from the same spot, because a phone is far more consistent relative to itself than in absolute terms. The reply is a voice note in the user's language, English or Hindi today, giving one clear next step: carry on, re-record soon, or ask a technician. It refuses to guess when a recording is poor, never names a diagnosis, and after a repair it checks whether the machine has returned to its normal. The full pipeline runs end-to-end today on simulated recordings. Our next step is the accelerometer-referenced validation protocol we have written.
