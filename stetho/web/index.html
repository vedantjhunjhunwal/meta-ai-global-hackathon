1 · Record safely

Safety first: never touch a running machine, never remove guards or covers, and keep loose clothing and hair away. Stand where you normally stand.
  1. Rest the phone on a brick or wall about 1 m away. A steady phone matters more than anything else.
  2. Machine in the centre, with some floor or wall visible around it. Stetho uses the still background to cancel hand shake.
  3. Same spot every time. Mark it with paint or a stone. Comparisons are only fair from the same spot.
  4. 10–15 seconds, sound on. Use slow-motion mode if your phone has it.
  5. First time? Save 3 recordings as a healthy reference on different days, ideally after a service.
